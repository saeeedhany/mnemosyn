For specialists

Review the atlas

If you work in neuroanatomy, neuroradiology, neurosurgery, neurology or teach anatomy, your review makes the atlas trustworthy for every student who uses it. It takes three steps.

Step 1

Read the review packet

A PDF listing the structures, boundaries and descriptions that need a specialist's eye, with screenshots from the app.

Download the packet (PDF)
Step 2

Answer the form

Mark what is right, what is wrong and what is missing. Review as much or as little as your time allows.

Open the review form
Step 3

Get credited

If you agree in the form, you're listed here with your specialty, affiliation and the parts you reviewed, and the structures you checked lose their Draft mark.
